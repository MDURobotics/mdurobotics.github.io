NextGen Tech Meet is held for the first time. About 30 companies from local
and regional industry exhibit and meet MDU students on engineering
programmes: Master of Science in Engineering, Bachelor of Science in
Engineering and master's students in years 1–5. Take the chance to talk about
degree projects, internships and future jobs.

The fair is held in Northvolt's former offices at Finnslätten and is organised
together with Castellum, who offer coffee.

**Audience:** Students and companies.

## Exhibitors

ABB, ABB Robotics, Alstom, Avalon, Batterihub, Castellum, Congere, Festo, GPV,
Haldrones AB, HiQ, Knightec, Lyten Labs, Mindflower, Prevas, Qtagg, Quintus,
Real Test, RSP, Scailab, Scania, Sejfo, Sigma Industry, TechSeed, Volvo CE,
Volvo Powertrain and Würth Elektronik Sweden AB.

## Getting here

Lugna Gatan 20, Finnslätten, Västerås. Parking is available at Castellum
sportcenter and on the other side of Lugna gatan (EasyPark, the first two
hours are free).
