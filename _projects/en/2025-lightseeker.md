## The problem

The researchers needed a rugged robot vehicle to test self-driving on, and one that meets the requirements of the European robot competition ARES Makeathon. The team first compared this with buying a ready-made vehicle and rebuilding it, but concluded that it was better to build their own from scratch.

## How it works

- **Sense** The vehicle is built so that different sensors can be mounted on it, depending on what the researchers want to test.
- **Plan** A person drives it remotely. The software is based on ROS 2, so that self-driving can be added later.
- **Act** Each wheel has its own motor inside the rim and its own suspension, so that the wheels stay in contact with the ground in rough terrain.
{: .sta}

## How did it go?

The vehicle was completed and met the requirements: it weighs 49 kg, carries at least 25 kg of load, runs for about an hour on one charge and climbs slopes steeper than 30 degrees. It is now a platform for further research on self-driving vehicles.

## Words to know

- **UGV:** Unmanned Ground Vehicle.
- **Hub motor:** an electric motor that sits inside the wheel. This leaves more room in the chassis.
- **ROS 2:** Robot Operating System, a software toolbox that many robots around the world are built on.

**Think about:** Why is it an advantage for each wheel to have its own suspension when driving in the forest?
